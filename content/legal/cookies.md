## We don't use cookies

**manthanam.in does not set any cookies** — not for analytics, advertising, social media or anything else. It also stores **nothing in your browser's local or session storage**, and while you browse it loads **nothing from other companies' servers**: the fonts, images and code all come from manthanam.in itself.

That is why there is **no cookie banner**: there is nothing for you to accept or reject.

We last checked this on 5 November 2026 by loading every page of the website in a fresh browser and recording every request, cookie and storage entry.

## The contact form

When you send a message, the form sends it to manthanam.in over an encrypted connection. The form does not set cookies or store your message in your browser.

## Manthanam's own staff

The private admin area (manthanam.in/admin), used only by Manthanam's team, sets **strictly necessary sign-in cookies** when a team member signs in, to keep them signed in. Visitors to the public website never receive them. See our [Privacy Policy](/privacy) for how your message is handled.

## Links to other websites

Some pages link to other sites, such as LinkedIn and Instagram profiles. Nothing is loaded from those sites until you click a link. Once you are on their site, their own cookie policies apply.

## If this changes

If we ever add analytics or any other non-essential cookies, we will **ask for your consent before setting them**, give you an equally easy way to refuse, and update this page first.
