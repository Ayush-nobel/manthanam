This policy explains how **Manthanam Research Foundation** ("Manthanam", "we", "us"), a registered non-governmental organisation based in New Delhi, India, handles personal data collected through **manthanam.in**. It is written to meet India's **Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023** and the **Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025**.

**Contact for anything in this policy:** [contact@manthanam.in](mailto:contact@manthanam.in)

## The short version

- You can read this website **without giving us any personal data**. It uses **no cookies, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking**, and loads nothing from other companies' servers while you browse.
- We only collect personal data when **you choose to write to us** (through the contact form or by email) or **register for one of our events**.
- We use it **only to reply to you or to run the event you registered for**. We never sell it, never use it for advertising, and never share it except with the service providers listed below.
- You can **ask us to see, correct or delete** your data at any time.

## Our legal basis

We process the personal data you send us on the basis of your **consent** (section 6 of the DPDP Act), which you give by ticking the consent box on the contact form, registration form or internship application form, or by writing to us. For a registrant under 18, we rely on the **verifiable consent of their parent or legal guardian** (section 9). You can **withdraw your consent at any time** by emailing [contact@manthanam.in](mailto:contact@manthanam.in); withdrawing is as easy as giving consent. Withdrawal does not affect processing already done, but we will stop using your data and delete it as described below.

## Who handles your data

We use a small number of service providers ("Data Processors") who handle data only on our instructions:

| Provider | What they do for us | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Supabase** | Hosts our private database, which stores contact-form messages, event registrations (including proof of payment) and internship applications; only Manthanam's signed-in team can see them | India (Mumbai) |

| **Zoho Mail and ZeptoMail** (sending, both Zoho services) | Sends the email confirming your registration, with your registration number, the email with payment details, and the email confirming an internship application, from a manthanam.in address, and forwards contact-form messages to our team | Zoho's data centres for our account |
| **Zoho Mail** | Hosts our email inbox, where enquiries and replies are kept | Zoho's data centres for our account |
| **Amazon Web Services** (Amazon CloudFront) | Hosts and delivers this website | Global network of servers |

Some of this processing happens **outside India**. The DPDP Act permits this unless the Government of India restricts transfers to a particular country; we will review our providers if that happens.

We do not sell, rent or trade personal data, and we do not share it with anyone else unless the law requires us to.

## How long we keep it

We keep your enquiry and our correspondence **only as long as we need to deal with it**, and **no longer than 12 months after our last exchange** — unless you ask us to keep in touch or the law requires us to keep it longer. After that we delete it from our database, from Formspree (if it went through Formspree) and from our inbox.

We keep **event registrations** until the event and its follow-up are finished, and **delete them within 12 months after the event** (for the International Youth Summit 2026, by 19 December 2027), unless the law requires us to keep something longer, for example a record of a payment. If you ask us to delete it sooner, we will (see below).

We keep **internship applications** for **12 months after we decide on them**, so we can contact you about a later opening, and then delete them. If you become an intern, we keep what we need to run the internship and to issue your certificate and letter of recommendation. Ask us at any time to delete your application sooner.

## Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you have the right to:

- **Access** — ask what personal data we hold about you and how we use it, and who we have shared it with.
- **Correct, complete or update** inaccurate or incomplete data.
- **Erase** your data — for example, to have your message and email address deleted.
- **Withdraw consent** at any time.
- **Nominate** someone to exercise these rights on your behalf if you die or become unable to do so.
- **Have your grievance addressed** by us.

**To use any of these rights**, email [contact@manthanam.in](mailto:contact@manthanam.in) with the subject "Privacy request" and tell us what you would like us to do. We may ask you to confirm you control the email address involved, so that we never disclose or delete someone else's data. We aim to respond within **30 days**, and in any case within the **90 days** the DPDP Rules allow.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the **Data Protection Board of India** after first raising the matter with us.

## Children

The contact form is for adults. If you are **under 18**, please ask a **parent or legal guardian** to write to us for you.

A parent or guardian can use the form by choosing "I am a parent or legal guardian writing on behalf of someone under 18" and giving the additional consent shown. Before we act on any information about the child, we will **verify that the person writing is an identifiable adult and the child's parent or guardian**, as the DPDP Rules require. We never track children or show them advertising. If we learn that someone under 18 has written to us directly without a parent or guardian, we will delete that message.

**Event registration** is open to people under 18 only with a parent or legal guardian. The registration form asks for the parent or guardian's details and consent when the registrant will be under 18 on the day of the event. Before we confirm the place, we contact the parent or guardian to **verify that they are an identifiable adult and the registrant's parent or guardian**. If we cannot verify this, we do not confirm the place and we delete the registration.

## Security

The whole website is served over **HTTPS**, and the contact and registration forms **only send your details over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection**. The registration spreadsheet is private to Manthanam's account and is not published anywhere. Access to enquiries is limited to the people at Manthanam who need it to reply. If a personal data breach affecting you occurs, we will inform you and the Data Protection Board of India as the law requires.

## Links to other websites

Our pages link to other sites (for example, our team members' LinkedIn and Instagram profiles). Those sites are not part of manthanam.in; nothing is loaded from them until you click a link, and once you follow one, their own privacy policies apply.

## Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle personal data, we will update this page and the date at the top. If a change affects data you have already given us, we will tell you before it applies.
