## We don't accept payments yet

**Manthanam Research Foundation does not currently accept any payments** — no donations, membership fees or event fees are collected through this website or otherwise online. If you are asked to pay "Manthanam" anywhere, please check with us first at [contact@manthanam.in](mailto:contact@manthanam.in).

We plan to accept **donations** and **fees for events and workshops** in future. Before we do, we will update this page with the payment provider we use and any details specific to it. The principles below will apply.

## Donations

Donations are voluntary and are **not refundable**, except where a payment was made **in error** — for example, a **duplicate payment** or an **incorrect amount**. If that happens, email us at [contact@manthanam.in](mailto:contact@manthanam.in) as soon as possible with the payment details, and we will refund the erroneous amount to the original payment method.

## Event and workshop fees

Fees for events and workshops are **refundable on request** made before the **cancellation deadline stated on that event's registration page**. To request a refund, email [contact@manthanam.in](mailto:contact@manthanam.in) with your name, the event and your payment details. Refunds go back to the original payment method.
